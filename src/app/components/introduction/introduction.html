Introduction

This interactive browser allows exploration of the functional effects of LRRK2 variants assessed in an HEK293 overexpression model, using Rab10 Thr73 phosphorylation as a readout of LRRK2 kinase pathway activity, expressed as fold change relative to wild-type LRRK2. Variants can be explored by protein domain, structural location and magnitude of kinase activation.

For details of the experimental methods, data normalisation and interpretation, please refer to the original publication: Cheung et al., Large-scale functional annotation establishes a reference framework for human LRRK2 variants.
